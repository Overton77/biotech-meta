BellLabs
========

Your Health In Your Own Hands.

BellLabs is the plan for an agentic biotech, biohacking, and longevity platform: a place where people can explore interventions, lab tests, devices, protocols, companies, evidence, practitioner playbooks, and personal biomarker journeys with the help of AI agents and a living biotech knowledge graph.

The core thesis is that health exploration and purchasing should evolve beyond search results, affiliate links, and isolated checkout flows. A product decision should carry its own reasoning trace, provenance, evidence bundle, safety caveats, personal context, and follow-up plan. The research is part of the purchase.

## North Star

BellLabs should become a biological operating system for self-directed health exploration. It is not a replacement for clinicians, diagnosis, or emergency care. It is a platform for structured inquiry, evidence tracking, protocol design, product evaluation, lab result interpretation support, and long-running personal experimentation.

The long-term pinnacle is a progressively richer "ME2" model: a user-owned, evidence-bound, continuously updated simulation of a person's health context. Full biological simulation is not currently available and may never exist at the granularity imagined by science fiction. BellLabs should still be built so every lab, wearable, protocol, purchase, symptom, document, and research thread moves the user closer to a useful personal model.
