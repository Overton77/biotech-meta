## Authority and evidence limits

The current semantic baseline is [catalog/schema.yaml](../catalog/schema.yaml), version 0.2.0, `status: provisional`, integrated through [proposal-index.md](./proposal-index.md). Accepted round rules and accepted kernel decisions coexist with provisional domain modules and open identity rules. This is not a wholly accepted final schema. Read [README.md](../README.md), [architecture.md](../architecture.md), [competency-questions.md](./competency-questions.md), [query-shapes.md](./query-shapes.md), [modules.yaml](./modules.yaml), [live-schema-alignment.md](./live-schema-alignment.md), and the round records together. Round headers may preserve earlier worker recommendations beneath the final integration decision.

The current [GraphQL schema](../current_biotech_schema.graphql) supplies existing type surfaces and failure-prone seams. It is read-only. [proposed-delta.graphql](../neo4j/proposed-delta.graphql) explicitly says it is an additive projection proposal and cannot change existing field directives through `extend type`; it is not a final executable replacement. [constraints.cypher](../neo4j/constraints.cypher) explicitly delegates cross-label, relationship-cardinality, and provenance rules to validation and ingestion logic. [OPEN-QUESTIONS.md](../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md) records unresolved modeling and live-stack facts.

The baseline was developed from a consumer-supplement slice. Its enduring product/material/evidence distinctions generalize, but that origin does not justify a forced economic taxonomy or an approved `Consumer Supplements` super-domain. “Drug,” “device,” “food,” “supplement,” jurisdictional authorization, purpose, and technology modality can be intersecting classifications rather than mutually exclusive trees. Source-grounded models should preserve these axes.

## Seam rules that prevent duplicate schemas

The existing manifest rule is decisive: every shared concept has exactly one owner and others reference it. Maintain an ownership registry with current owner, proposed sub-owner, imported endpoint surface, write authority and CQ/failure rationale. Log each disagreement with proposed alternatives, sources, failing case, affected CQs, migration implications, resolving owner and disposition. The synthesizer resolves or defers; worker-local duplicate types never enter the merged schema.

- **Protocol collisions:** study `ProtocolVersion` describes research design; public `ProtocolEdition` describes a reusable practice template; private `ProtocolAdoptionVersion`/`ProtocolInUse` live in PCS. Protocol scheduling cannot become actual personal adherence data. Protocol owner coordinates dose with materials/products, instruments with device/diagnostics, biomedical targets with concepts, evidence with studies/provenance, and public author/role links with organizations. Composite subprotocol edition binding, branch conditions and step identities across editions remain explicit research questions.
- **Material collisions:** live `Compound` maps to catalog `ChemicalSubstance`; `CompoundForm` splits across `ChemicalForm`, actual `IngredientMaterial`, and dosage form. Live `Material` and `Ingredient` need a per-use disposition; neither becomes a second canonical chemical ontology. Product owns contextual ingredient components; substance owner owns material identities; manufacturing owns material usage in process after endpoint agreement.
- **Result collisions:** diagnostic measurement/result, lot analytical result, study outcome result, public protocol observation and private personal measurement each retain context and owner. Share units/measurand and evidence interfaces; do not collapse them into one generic result because they contain numeric values.
- **Outcome collisions:** concepts owner owns `Outcome`/`Condition`; studies owns `OutcomeDefinition` and result inference; diagnostics owns `Metric`/`Biomarker`; protocol owns `Target`/`FunctionalGoal`. None supplies a generic “works” edge.
- **Source/media collisions:** provenance owns Source/snapshot/locator; documents owns text rendering/chunks; narrative owns work/episode/series/speaker acts; assets owns depictions/variants/rights. A single video may be linked across these surfaces, with explicit distinction between work, rendition, immutable capture and curated asset. Do not substitute a time string on an episode for a locator on a captured rendition.
- **Publication collisions:** studies owns `Publication` as scientific artifact reporting/analyzing a study/dataset; document owner supplies its captured document surface and provenance owner its Source/snapshot/locator. One paper should not produce incompatible owner-local DOI identities.
- **Regulatory/quality collisions:** regulator owns jurisdiction/action/status and source response; quality owns test/certification scope; product owns formulation/variant; manufacturing owns capability. Facility registration, cGMP claim, certificate, GRAS response and drug approval cannot imply each other.
- **Claim truth collisions:** Claim identity, source ClaimOccurrence, source capture fidelity, support adjudication, evidence-strength assessment, applicability assessment and private ranking remain different. One Assertion has at most one asserter; ClaimOccurrence has exactly one. Sources jointly corroborate through distinct occurrences, never a multi-speaker Assertion.

## Research and output requirements for later workers

This turn's findings are repository-grounded only. Later workers should retrieve primary records to decide properties, not write generic sector surveys. Use available Firecrawl/Tavily for first-party pages, PubMed for bibliographic/full-text links, ClinicalTrials.gov registration/results and official labels/agency records/filings/company or investor pages. Verify actual tool availability before calling; if unavailable, record the tool failure and use authorized official-web/manual fallback. A search extract is not a complete fetched snapshot; preserve retrieval/capture completeness and never invent quotations or retrieved evidence.

Each worker's packet must contain: exact baseline digest and owned/imported type inventory; natural domain/subdomain and scope; source manifest with authoritative purpose, retrieval time, capture kind, version and locators; evidence-linked identity/state/archetype decisions; node/property/edge/edge-property recommendation with data types/enums/cardinality/units/missingness/privacy/time/relationship class; CQ/query-shape/failing-case mapping for every addition; positive, negative and minimal-pair fixtures with expected query results; operational enforcement/index/retrieval needs and library/edition dependencies; migration effects; unresolved ownership ledger entries. Example SDL fragments are recommendations only until Fable merges actual full definitions.

Adversarial minimums: UNKNOWN versus UNMEASURED versus NOT_REPORTED versus BELOW_DETECTION versus measured ABSENT versus boolean FALSE; late fact versus source correction versus validity ending; same name/different identity; marketed versus approved; label versus measured versus calculated dose; study-material versus current product; biomarker versus clinical benefit; mouse mechanism versus measured human effect; sponsor read versus editorial statement; speaker practice versus recommendation; asset depiction versus evidentiary claim; private protocol execution versus shared public template.
