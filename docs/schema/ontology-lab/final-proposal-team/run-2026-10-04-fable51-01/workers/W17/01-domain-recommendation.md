## 1. Boundary

The domain answers two question families: **"what is known about harm from using X, from which records, and how was absence of reports handled"** (CQ-ST-06, CQ-AX-24) and **"is the use of X restricted in a stated scope, by whom, at what directive strength, and is that restriction stated, removed, unknown or unrecorded at a viewpoint"** (CQ-RC-06, CQ-RC-07). It supplies the shared constraint identity a private recommendation blocks on, but it never decides a block (the private policy does, W23), never holds a person's conditions or medications (private store), never counts adverse events (W09 `AdverseEventResult`), never grades evidence strength or applicability (W10), and never states protocol step conditions (W16 `Constraint`).

| Subdomain | Elements | Archetype | Identity basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Harm concepts | `AdverseEffect` | Entity | BellLabs uid; MedDRA or other terminology codes as `Identifier` records; never merged with a W03 `Condition` by name |
| Signal evaluation | `SafetySignal` | **EvidenceAssessment** | one immutable, method-versioned evaluation; re-evaluation = new node `SUPERSEDES` old |
| Source statements | `ContraindicationAssertion`, `InteractionAssertion` (labels `…:Assertion`); agency signal listings as generic `Assertion` | Assertion | kernel contract; one subject, one object, at most one asserter |
| Shared constraint identity | `UseConstraint` | Entity | canonical scope tuple hashed as `identityKeyHash` (uc-key/v1) |
| Edges | `HAS_SAFETY_SIGNAL`, `RELATES_TO_EFFECT`, `RELATES_TO_CONDITION`, `SIGNAL_BASED_ON`, `CONSTRAINS_USE_OF`, `CONSTRAINT_SCOPE` (structural); `RESOLVES_TO_CONSTRAINT`, `REPORTS_SAFETY_SIGNAL` (derived); `AFFECTS_ORGAN` (structural curated, shared meaning with W03) | | |

Identity vs state vs artifact vs occurrence: a harm concept and a constraint scope are **Entities** with no truth value; what a label, an agency or a review *says* is an **Assertion**; BellLabs' evaluation of AE counts and listings is an **EvidenceAssessment**; reported AE counts are W09 **InformationArtifacts**; the label page, the safety communication and the AEMS quarterly page are **SourceSnapshots** (the AEMS page is edited in place: its "Additional Information (as of October 24, 2025)" column changes without a new URL, so each observation is a snapshot). No VersionedState is needed: constraint change over time is carried by bitemporal assertions with `SUPERSEDES {VALIDITY_BOUNDED}`.

## 2. Findings from records retrieved in this run (03-source-manifest.md)

1. **Label directives are scoped and graded, and the grade is not binary.** ZOCOR (DailyMed setid `8f55d5de…`, "Revised: 8/2023") states CONTRAINDICATIONS scoped by co-exposure ("Concomitant use of cyclosporine, danazol or gemfibrozil") and by condition ("Acute liver failure or decompensated cirrhosis"); dose-scoped limits ("Patients taking Verapamil, Diltiazem, or Dronedarone: Do not exceed ZOCOR 10 mg once daily"); a population- and co-exposure-dose-scoped recommendation ("Concomitant use of ZOCOR with lipid-modifying dosages of niacin is not recommended in Chinese patients"; niacin "≥1 gram/day"); and, in the same row, a weaker directive for everyone else ("For non-Chinese patients, consider if the benefit … outweighs the increased risk"); plus "Avoid grapefruit juice". One label therefore yields five directive strengths, three scope kinds and two dose referents (the constrained drug's dose and the co-exposure's dose). A boolean "contraindicated" flag or a single score loses all of this; a free-text "interaction summary" (live `SafetySignal.interactionSummary`) cannot be blocked on.
2. **Interaction facts and directives are different statements, often in one sentence.** "Grapefruit juice can raise the plasma levels of simvastatin" (interaction, POSITIVE) vs "Avoid grapefruit juice" (directive). Asher et al. (AFP 2017, PMID 28762712): kava "results of two in vitro studies suggest the potential to inhibit CYP2C9 … warfarin" (interaction, **UNKNOWN**, in vitro only) followed by "should be closely monitored … or instructed not to use" (directive MONITOR); "Two human trials have demonstrated no effect of American ginseng on … indinavir" (interaction **NEGATIVE**, a studied absence). The review grades its own recommendations with SORT ("Drug interactions with goldenseal and St. John's wort are highly likely … | C", legend "C = consensus, disease-oriented evidence, usual practice, expert opinion, or case series") even where "Several clinical trials report consistent findings": a source's grade is what the source said, not a BellLabs strength.
3. **An NIH ODS monograph gives no evidence level and a non-avoidance directive.** Vitamin K: "People taking warfarin … need to maintain a consistent intake of vitamin K" — the constraint is consistency, not avoidance (level MAINTAIN_CONSISTENT_INTAKE). A model with only "avoid/contraindicated" would mis-block every vitamin K-containing food product.
4. **Contraindications are removed, not corrected.** FDA Drug Safety Communication 2021-07-20: "These changes include removing the contraindication against using these medicines in all pregnant patients", with an exception ("a small group of very high-risk pregnant patients") and a new weaker directive ("most patients should stop statins once they learn they are pregnant"); breastfeeding: "Patients should not breastfeed when taking a statin". The earlier contraindication was not an error; it ended. This is `SUPERSEDES {VALIDITY_BOUNDED}` plus a NEGATIVE assertion at the CONTRAINDICATED level and a POSITIVE one at AVOID — and "contraindication removed" must never read as "safe in pregnancy" (FI-W17-03).
5. **A signal announcement is explicitly not a finding.** FDA AEMS (formerly FAERS) quarterly pages: "The appearance of a drug … on a quarterly report does not mean that FDA has concluded that the drug … has the listed risk or that FDA has identified a causal relationship". Rows move through states the agency names: "FDA is evaluating the need for regulatory action"; labeling "updated … to include information about hyperkalemia" (daptomycin); "FDA determined that no action was necessary at the time based on available information" (lenvatinib/sorafenib, tumor lysis syndrome). That is a lifecycle (`SignalStatus`) attached to an evaluation with inputs, not a property of a timeless "signal" entity, and "closed, no action" is not "no risk".
6. **"No serious AEs reported" carries an undescribed collection method.** Dellinger 2017 (PMC5701244): "there were no serious AEs reported during this clinical study"; Methods: "Safety parameters measured included … self-reported AEs" — the paper never says whether AEs were elicited systematically (W09-D07 reached the same reading: `NOT_DESCRIBED`). Relatedness was "assessed" but the assessor is not named. The registry record NCT02678611 has `has_results: false` (ClinicalTrials.gov MCP), so no registry AE module exists to settle the method; the direct API was BLOCKED (403). The trial also **excluded** "currently taking lipid lowering drugs", so its AE table cannot speak to NR + statin co-use at all (applicability, W10).

## 4. Alternatives considered

|---|---|---|
| SafetySignal archetype | (a) **Entity concept with assessments attached** (live shape: a "signal" node holding severity, evidenceStrength, frequency; EvidenceStrengthAssessment/Synthesis attached) | Failing case F-1 (fixture 02): the NRPT GI signal is evaluated by `bl-safety-signal/0.1` as POTENTIAL over two AE rows, then by `0.2` as INSUFFICIENT_DATA after adding the 1X arm and a systematic zero. Attaching assessments leaves the Entity's own fields as unattributed verdicts (INV-209, V-213). ## 5. Smallest recommended model

Five node types (two live refined, three catalog candidates promoted with fixtures), five enums (one live, four new and each tied to a fixture: `SignalStatus`, `ConstraintLevel`, `ConstraintScopeRole`, `SafetySubjectRole`), three unions (one live successor, two new), three relationship-property types (one registry successor, two new), the five registry relationship types (three structural, one structural-curated shared with W03, one derived) and four new relationship types (`SIGNAL_BASED_ON`, `CONSTRAINS_USE_OF`, `CONSTRAINT_SCOPE`, `RESOLVES_TO_CONSTRAINT`), three registered-predicate candidates (`USE_CONSTRAINED_IN`, `USE_CONSTRAINED_WITH`, `INTERACTS_WITH`) and one agency predicate (`IDENTIFIES_POTENTIAL_SAFETY_SIGNAL`), and thirteen validators. Nothing about a person enters the shared graph; the private side is an external contract (06 section 4). Not proposed: a `ProductRecall` or `SafetyCommunication` type (a communication is a W20 `Document` of type SAFETY_COMMUNICATION and its statements are Assertions; recalls are W12/W13 regulatory/quality records — no W17 CQ needs a recall node), a MedDRA hierarchy (licensed terminology; codes stay `Identifier`s), a disproportionality-statistics model (no CQ; method lives in `methodVersion`).
