Dr. Sinclair personally takes a precursor to NAD+ called NMN (nicotinamide mononucleotide). The body uses NMN to make NAD+. He notes that taking NMN can double NAD+ levels in the blood within about two weeks, fueling the sirtuin defense system.
Dr. Sinclair is on the scientific advisory board for Metro Biotech, a biotechnology company that is currently investigating NMN as a new drug.
