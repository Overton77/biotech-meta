## What the real records together establish

- Same LOINC (4548-4), three different public procedures (Mayo HPLC on D-100; Labcorp "Roche Tina Quant"; Quest TINIA), three different printed intervals (4.0-5.6; 4.8-5.6; `<5.7`). The forbidden implication [SHARES_LOINC_CODE, SAME_ASSAY_VERSION] is observed in real data, not only in synthetic fixtures.
- Specimen acceptance differs per lab for the same LOINC (Labcorp accepts sodium fluoride tubes; Quest rejects them): specimen type belongs to AssayVersion.
- Labs print reference intervals and guideline decision limits together; Quest's printed "reference range" is a guideline-based decision limit; Quest itself tells readers to use the range on the laboratory report (supports INV-302 "interval printed at report time").
- Patient-facing reports carry less assay detail than test-menu pages: the Labcorp sample report prints no method, no kit, no LOINC.
- No public record consulted states a software version or an EP28 derivation kind.
