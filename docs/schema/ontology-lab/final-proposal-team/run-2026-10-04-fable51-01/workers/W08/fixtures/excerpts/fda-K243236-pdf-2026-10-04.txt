[p5] The WHOOP ECG Feature is a software-only medical mobile application integrated into the consumer (non-device) WHOOP System. It consists of three medical device modules: ECG Strap Module, ECG Phone Module, and ECG Cloud Module.
[p5] The ECG Strap Module firmware is integrated within the WHOOP Strap’s firmware. The ECG Strap Module firmware contains the FDA-cleared B-Secur HeartKey Software Library (K200884)
