Updates are delivered through firmware, meaning you benefit automatically without purchasing new hardware.
What's new — as of July 2026?
WHOOP just delivered another across-the-board improvement to heart rate accuracy. The biggest gains are in the activities that are hardest for any wrist-worn sensor to measure like weightlifting and functional fitness, along with HIIT, cycling, and golf. Everyday activities like walking got more accurate too.
This update does not impact sleep, including Heart Rate Variability (HRV) and Resting Heart Rate (RHR).
WHOOP measures heart rate using advanced optical sensors with photoplethysmography (PPG) technology.
While raw data is captured using the sensor hardware, an algorithm is required to estimate heart rate.
