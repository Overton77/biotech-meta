## Settled research questions

1. **Does UNII/GSRS distinguish NR chloride from NR?** Yes. There are two approved records, 8XM2XT8VWI (Salt or Solvate; CAS 23111-00-4; PubChem 90480033) and 0I8H2M0L7N (Active Moiety; CAS 1341-23-7; PubChem 439924; ChEBI 15927; DrugBank DB14933). They are linked by `PARENT->SALT/SOLVATE` and `ACTIVE MOIETY` (M-01, M-02, M-07). No GSRS record matches "NIAGEN" (M-20). The crystal form is not a separate substance record.
2. **How are botanical extract ratio and standardization stated?** On an authority monograph the statement has three parts: the native ratio range "DER 35-67:1", the solvent with concentration "acetone 60% m/m", and an "adjusted to" range for an analytical measurand ("flavonoids calculated as flavone glycosides") that differs by pharmacopoeia and edition (M-10, M-11, M-12). The first-party Mosaic label (M-08) states neither a ratio nor a standardization, only the plant part and "providing …" lists. Its absence is recorded as unknown.
3. **A probiotic strain with a deposit identifier.** L. rhamnosus GG is ATCC 53103, which BacDive equates with CCUG 34291 and LMG 18243. ATCC BAA-3227 is accessioned progeny. The NCBI taxids are 568703 (strain) and 47715 (species) (M-13, M-14, M-15, M-16).
4. **A branded material governed by a specification (NIAGEN).** Two ChromaDex-proposed specifications differ (2015: purity 95–102 wt%, water ≤1%; 2019: ≥90 wt%, water ≤2.0%; M-05, M-06). Separate regulatory specifications exist (EU Union list ≥90%, M-23; FSANZ assay 90.0–103 w/w%, M-24). These are W11/W12 specification records and must not be collapsed into the material.
