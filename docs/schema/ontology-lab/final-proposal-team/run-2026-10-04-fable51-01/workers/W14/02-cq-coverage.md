# W14 CQ coverage matrix

Answerability codes follow competency-questions.md section 1.2 (A = answerable, Q = answerable with qualifications, X = needs information outside the graph). New CQs are **candidates** (`CQ-IP-Cnn`). They are not existing ids. The handoff (section 2) requires "a focused patent scope/licensing CQ … before expansion"; CQ-IP-C01 and CQ-IP-C02 are those focused CQs, and nothing beyond them and C03–C05 is expanded. Query ids Q-01…Q-12 are in `fixtures/w14-cq-queries.cypher`; expected rows are in `06-fixtures-and-queries.md`.
