## Decisive findings for modeling

1. A patent-level aggregator status ("Active", S1) coexists with a claim-level appellate holding of ineligibility (S5). Status must be per right and per claim, and must name its asserter (→ `IpRightStatus`).
2. The license names **specific** members plus a verbatim rule ("any Foreign Patents issuing therefrom …", S2), and the displayed family (S1) is a different set. Coverage is exactly what the license asserts (→ V-W14-05).
3. Two agreements between the same parties have different capture completeness (S2 vs S3). Terms must carry their own missingness (→ `*ReportedStatus`, V-W14-13).
4. The US mark record lists a related international registration (S6). One mark text means several rights (→ `Trademark` per jurisdiction, V-W14-08).
5. The mark owner of record is a subsidiary (ChromaDex, Inc., S6), while the filing's ingredient supplier is the group "Company" (S7). Owner ≠ supplier, and group ≠ member (→ V-W14-12; W01 `[GROUP_LEVEL_ROLE, MEMBER_COMPANY_ROLE]`).
