## Case packet: what the decisive records settled

1. **Concept versus regulatory identity (S1–S3).** One product page carries three identities: proper name, tradename and manufacturer. Two STNs carry different indications that change over time, and the orphan designation predates the tradename. Treatment identity therefore holds only the concept: proper name and development code as identifiers, and modality. Approval, indication, STN and dates are W13's. Product is W04's.
2. **Modality is multi-valued and differs from registry typing (S2, S6, S7).** "autologous genome edited hematopoietic stem cell-based gene therapy" and "a cellular gene therapy" describe one concept that registries type as BIOLOGICAL. Delandistrogene moxeparvovec is typed GENETIC.
3. **Procedure as a component of a concept versus a study-specific step (S2, S7).** The label states that apheresis collection is part of the CASGEVY concept. HORIZON's plasmapheresis is a trial-specific preparatory step.
4. **Offering versus definition versus listing versus performance (S9, S10).** The offerer page supports an OFFERS_PROCEDURE assertion and W15 listing and price records. The tests bundled into the offer are offer terms, not procedure definition. The benefit wording is a claim. The session count is a literal.
5. **Designation versus approval, per sponsor (S3–S5).** "Designated/Approved" is two facts. "Designated/Designation Withdrawn or Revoked" plus "Not FDA Approved for Orphan Indication" for one sponsor coexists with another sponsor's approved product of the same substance for the same condition.
6. **Codes versus identity (S11).** "Pheresis of Plasma" spans donation and exchange, and Single versus Multiple is a performance count.
