# CH-W16 protocols: Wave 5 adversarial review

Challenger: W16 role (protocols), Opus 5.5, run `run-2026-10-04-fable51-01`, 2026-10-04. Attack surface (brief `03-wave5-challenger-brief.md`): adherence inferred from adoption, dependency loops read as repetition, midpoint cadence, a third-party report minted as an edition, and private execution stored in `Observation`. The objections were tested against the assembled artifacts: `final_biotech_schema_proposal.graphql` (W16 banner, the W07 `DiagnosticResult` interface and the W23 boundary), `final_biotech_schema_operations.cypher`, the `fixtures-final/` set and the compiled validation suite.

The mutations and their undos are in `CH-W16-protocols.cypher`, which has 118 statements. All 118 pass `EXPLAIN` on the instance.

## Method and environment

- **Database.** An isolated embedded Neo4j 5.26.31 Community instance with APOC (instance c5), loaded with the final operations file. The `run-cypher.mjs` harness ran every statement in its own transaction.
- **Validators.** Every counterexample ran against the full suite of 280 statements:
  - `docs/schema/neo4j/validation.cypher`
  - W00 `validation-corrections.cypher` (V-xxx r)
  - W07 `w07-validation.cypher`
  - W23 `validation-w23.cypher`
  - W16 `00-w16-validators.cypher`

  Parameters were `validation-params.json` merged with `qs-params.json`. A focused bundle of 42 statements was used for quick diffs.
- **Procedure.** For each section: empty the database, load the base fixture named in the `.cypher` file, run the suite, apply the mutation, run the suite again and diff the row counts per validator. Then apply the undo and check that the counts are back to the baseline. Every undo was checked this way, and no `ch-r` uid was left behind.
- **Reading the results.**
  - **NOT caught** means that no validator flags the defect for its own reason.
  - Three generic validators fire just as much on the legitimate fixture records. Their movement is recorded and is not counted as a catch:
    - V-110: no capture-fidelity adjudication.
    - The reproducible-locator check: the fixture's TEXT_QUOTE locators have no `quoteHash`.
    - V-108, V-108r and V-509: exclusive-attachment overlap review, which already flags the honest SNAPSHOT_DIFF pair in fixture 02.

## What held

- **Third-party provenance label.** V-530p catches an edition self-labelled THIRD_PARTY_REPORTED (fixture 02 negative) and an edition without provenance (the translated fixture).
- **Adoption → adherence, canonical name.** `FOLLOWS_ALL_STEPS` derived from an `ADOPTED_PROTOCOL_EDITION` input is caught by V-112 and V-112r (FORBIDDEN_IMPLICATION_AMONG_DERIVATION_INPUTS). The execution names V-534p knows are caught (`FOLLOWS`, `ADOPTED_IN_PRIVATE`, `OMITTED_STEP`, `adherence`, `omittedByUser`; fixtures 06 and 07).
- **Loops of ordering kinds.** V-528p catches self-dependency and 2-cycles when every edge carries REQUIRES_PRIOR_COMPLETION or REQUIRES_RESULT_OF (fixture 03 and CH-R-02a). V-527p catches self-loops and dependencies that leave the edition.
- **Ranges.** V-529p catches inverted ranges; V-529b catches a digit-form "3 to 6 months" collapsed to min = max (fixture 10).
- **ABOUT_CONDITION.** V-536p catches an ABOUT_CONDITION with a `derivationRule` and no assertion (CH-R-08a). W00 V-505r catches an asserted ABOUT_CONDITION whose cited Assertion's subject is not the edge start (CH-R-08b), and catches an `ADOPTS` edge citing an Assertion of another predicate or object.
- **Private uids, labels and collapse.** V-521 and V-521r catch any `hu:private-` value on an Observation (CH-R-07 control). V-520 and V-524 catch `:PersonalMeasurement` on an Observation and private-store labels (fixture 07). V-113 catches shared→private edges.
- **COMPARED_TO and INDICATES_CONDITION.**
  - W00 V-112r catches a rule-only COMPARED_TO across assay versions.
  - V-112 catches INDICATES_CONDITION without a citation.
  - V-302, V-302r and V-309 work once the DiagnosticResult label is present (CH-R-12c).
- **Duplicate stepKey within an edition on HAS_PROTOCOL_STEP.** Caught by V-525p and V-525r (CH-R-13c). CONDITIONAL without APPLIES_WHEN is caught regardless of the step edge.
- **Projection fidelity and contradictory kinds.** V-539p holds for HAS_PROTOCOL_EDITION projections of the right type. V-531p catches MUTUALLY_EXCLUSIVE combined with an ordering kind or CONCURRENT_WITH, and catches an exclusive pair that is both ESSENTIAL.
- **Schema and constraints.**
  - Every W16 case loads on the final operations constraints.
  - The SDL has no type, field or enum value for adoption, adherence or deviation.
  - `Protocol.currentSteps` and `Observation.comparedTo` are `@settable(false,false)`, so neither can be written through the API. Cypher writers are not limited by this; see CH-R-14.

## Untestable or not run here

- **GraphQL.** No GraphQL round trip was run for these cases. Field-to-edge mapping was read from the SDL: `ProtocolEdition.steps` = HAS_PROTOCOL_STEP, `Protocol.currentSteps` = HAS_CURRENT_PROTOCOL_STEP. The empty-steps result in CH-R-13 is a Cypher count over that edge.
- **Enterprise companion** (`final_biotech_schema_operations.enterprise.cypher`). Not run (Community only). By reading it: it would reject CH-R-09b (`protocol_step_requirement_level_exists`) and the translated fixture's editions without `changeProvenance` (`protocol_edition_change_provenance_exists`), so that fixture would fail to load on Enterprise. It has no relationship-property existence constraint for `DEPENDS_ON.dependencyKind` or `HAS_PROTOCOL_STEP.orderIndex`, so CH-R-02b and CH-R-11 would pass there too.
- **Service-enforced rules.** CH-R-06's real-world mitigation, that the private-store writer holds no shared-graph credential, is a deployment rule and cannot be tested in a single-database fixture. The validator proposed above is the testable part.
- **CH-R-04 is a definition gap.** No validator can detect a missing anchor. The evidence is the probe query's three disagreeing windows.
