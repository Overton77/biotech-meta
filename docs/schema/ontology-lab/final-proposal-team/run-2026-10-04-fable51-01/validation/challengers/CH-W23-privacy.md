# CH-W23 Wave 5 Challenger: privacy and access (W23 role)

Challenger: Opus 5.5 in the W23 role, run `run-2026-10-04-fable51-01`, 2026-10-04. Attack surface: the **assembled** artifacts, meaning `docs/schema/final_biotech_schema_proposal.graphql`, `docs/schema/neo4j/final_biotech_schema_operations.cypher`, `docs/schema/neo4j/validation.cypher` (the compiled suite as it stands), `validation/query-shapes-extracted.cypher` and the six translated fixtures in `validation/fixtures-final/`. Mutations, probes and undo are in `CH-W23-privacy.cypher`. The GraphQL operations are in the JSON block in section 4.

**Execution.** Everything below was run on an isolated embedded Neo4j 5.26.31 Community instance with APOC core (instance `c2`). The instance was preloaded with the final operations file, the fixtures-final set and the backfill. I loaded W23 `00-shared-base.cypher` (23/23 OK) and `04-uid-redirect.cypher` (5/5 OK) on top. I did not load W23's negative fixtures 10–13: W23 already ran them, and they would have mixed with my own probes. I ran a baseline of `validation.cypher` (174/174 statements with `validation-params.json`) and `workers/W23/fixtures/validation-w23.cypher` (12/12). Both suites ran again after the attacks, and the tables below compare the two runs. GraphQL ran through `@neo4j/graphql` 7.6.3 (harness `roundtrip.mjs`). The final SDL builds in about 45 s, with 476 query and 530 mutation root fields. After the UNDO block ran, the database was back to the baseline: 562 nodes, and both suites gave the same rows as before the attacks.

**Tiers defined by the proposal** (`AccessTier`, W23 04 §6): PUBLIC_ANSWER, OPERATOR_AUDIT, AGENT_PROJECTION, OWNER_PRIVATE. W23 says how PUBLIC_ANSWER is to be enforced (D-W23-13, 07 §2): through "compiled queries (QS-5b/QS-6a with allow-lists) or a generated tier sub-schema; **not** the operator GraphQL endpoint". The assembled proposal ships only the operator SDL. It ships no tier sub-schema. Its compiled shapes QS-5b, QS-6a and QS-8 still use the deny-list (QS-5b, QS-6a) or no instance filter at all (QS-8). The V-W23-01…10 validators are not in `validation.cypher`. Report 03 says they "are compiled into the final suite at Wave 6". In the tables below, "final suite" means `validation.cypher` and "W23 suite" means `validation-w23.cypher`.

- **RC-3. Public-person and participant rules only check that the edge is well formed. They never check where the record came from.** This drives CH-P-08, 10 and 11.

## 3. What held

- **D-012 / A9 at the type level.** The final SDL defines no private-personal type, no enum value, no `hu:private-` value and no private relationship (TIER-4b validation error; grep of the SDL). The 20 `:PrivateRecord` nodes that `fixtures-final/recommendation-snapshot.cypher` loads are unreachable through GraphQL: no generated type matches their label sets, and `entities`/`actorIdentities` returned none of them.
- **The W23 suite catches what it was built for.** V-W23-01a (OWNER_PRIVATE tier, INCLUDED_FOR_OWNER, PUBLIC class), V-W23-03 (wrong CITES_ASSESSMENT endpoint, traced answer without citation), V-W23-05 (RECORDS from an INTERNAL Person), V-W23-07 (ranking policy used as use authorization), V-W23-09 (laundered `PRIVATE-PERSONAL`, `synthetic`) and V-W23-10 (prefixed private uid in a relationship list) each fired on my mutations. Compiling them into the final suite would close CH-P-09, CH-P-13 and part of CH-P-12/14.
- **V-112 catches private uids on derived edges, indirectly.** Every uid in `derivedFromAssertionUids`/`derivedFromAssessmentUids` must resolve to a live Assertion or EvidenceAssessment, so private uids on derived edges are reported, even in case-mangled form (CH-P-07a/b).
- **The allow-list compiled traversal (W23 Q-PP-1) held.** From A2 (now WAS_GENERATED_BY an INTERNAL Activity) it reached 24 PUBLIC nodes with no policy, lineage or AnswerRecord. From the chp-09 Observation it cut the INTERNAL recorder. D-W23-05's allow-list is the correct mechanism. The proposal does not use it in QS-5b, QS-6a or QS-8 (CH-P-15).
- **A union-pruned PUBLIC sub-schema builds** (sketch: session scratchpad `harness/chw23-public-subset-fixed.mjs`, which is W23 `checks/public-subset.mjs` plus a `UnionTypeDefinition` visitor that drops excluded members) on @neo4j/graphql 7.6.3 against the final SDL in about 24 s, with 0 mutations and no INTERNAL type, `privacyClass` or `mongoResearchRunId`. Type-level exclusion of PolicyVersion, AnswerRecord, DecisionCriterion and Activity is achievable once the derivation prunes union members (CH-P-01 fix).
- **The Enum type fails closed on read.** Selecting `privacyClass` on a row stored as `private-personal`, `synthetic` or `PRIVATE-PERSONAL` raises a GraphQL error rather than returning it. This is an accident, not a control: omitting the field returns the row.
