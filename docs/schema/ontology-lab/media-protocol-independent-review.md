## Media: two distinct needs, with shared links

The live schema already contains substantial visual infrastructure (lines 2199–2569): IMAGE, VIDEO, AUDIO, diagrams, microscopy/medical images, molecular structures, 3D assets; format/purpose/generation/variant enums; DEPICTS, EXPLAINS, EVIDENCES and annotation coordinates; rights strings; byte and perceptual hashes; dimensions and duration. `MediaSubject` includes Product, Organization, Mechanism, Pathway, Condition, Outcome, Biomarker, Protocol, Claim and many other identities/concepts. Preserve that breadth when investigating useful target unions. Do not reduce media to product photos.

Quality fields (`qualityScore`, `authenticityScore`) and `isPrimarySource` currently exist, but catalog integration leaves media a **seam** (proposal-index §2). A future worker must supply a CQ or concrete failure for each retained/refined property. A generic score cannot automatically become an evidence verdict; quality for display, authenticity, extraction accuracy, and evidence strength require distinct documented methods where scored. Newly proposed display questions should be labelled candidate CQ extensions, not misrepresented as existing CQ-CL questions. Include a high-resolution concept diagram versus a low-resolution product image and an edited scientific figure versus an original capture in the case packet.

Human-made narrative sources are already grounded in CQ-CL-01–09 and CQ-PV-01–06 ([competency questions](./competency-questions.md), §5). `Episode` is a work; separate `Source` renditions link `RENDITION_OF` that work (catalog `claims_and_documents` and `provenance`). Series, channels, platforms, segments, transcripts, author/speaker roles, topics and exact spans must remain usable. Presentation slides can be Documents (`INVESTOR_PRESENTATION`, `CONFERENCE_PRESENTATION`) while a recorded talk may be an Episode; deciding when both denote representations of one work requires evidence and an identity seam, not a blanket merge.

Round 0006 D1–D8 grounds the five provenance states, rendition-specific media time, transcription and extraction lineage, corrections, retelling drift and sponsorship disclosures. An audio feed with dynamically inserted ads and its video rendition must not share timecodes automatically. A content correction from the publisher differs from correcting a mis-transcription: the former can leave the originally spoken assertion intact; the latter supersedes an inaccurate capture. A source's practice report is not a recommendation; mentioning a product is not endorsing it; a financial tie is neither support nor contradiction. Media content records what somebody represented, not truth by itself.

`SourceSnapshot` immutability, `contentHashBasis`, `captureCompleteness`, stored/archive location, and selectors are shared provenance interfaces. TEXT_POSITION binds to a DocumentTextVersion; MEDIA_TIME binds to the captured rendition; IMAGE_REGION can use MediaAnnotation. A new locator `REANCHORS` an older span on a new capture; reanchoring must not mutate the historical citation. Rights/usage policy is materially unfinished: OPEN-QUESTIONS priority 2 ecosystem item 1 names PolicyVersion vocabulary and unreviewed ODRL. Separate asset license/attribution and permitted byte reuse from policy permission to quote/summarize/use an assertion. Unknown rights are not permission. Do not invent legal conclusions or claim ODRL has been researched.

## Protocols: full public structure, separate personal execution

The live protocol section (lines 1756–2040) already has schedule patterns, cadence units/intervals, days and time of day, cycles, as-needed/continuous indicators, duration bounds, subprotocols, ordered steps, dependencies, `USES` with DoseMetadata, `EMPLOYS`, constraints, goals, measurements, adjustment rules and results. A future protocol worker must audit these actual fields and preserve useful source-expressible semantics rather than replace them with only a protocol name and text.

The accepted refinement is explicit (catalog `protocols`, round 0008 §6, CQ-PR-01–06): Protocol is enduring identity; ProtocolEdition is an immutable observed/published VersionedState attached by a bitemporal asserted episode; ProtocolStep has stable lineage `stepKey`, immutable payload, requirement level/basis and explicit notReportedFields. Dependencies distinguish prior completion, result requirement, concurrency and mutual exclusion. Constraints distinguish applies-when, contraindicated-when and requires-before, with population/condition/measurement/phase/time/co-intervention kinds. Scheduling must support bounded ranges (every 3–6 months is not a midpoint), repetitions, cycles, durations, doses and routes where source-stated. Conditional branching and repeated subprotocol execution require source packets and query fixtures to determine whether existing dependency/constraint fields suffice; they are not accepted new type decisions yet.

Important naming seam: study `ProtocolVersion` is an InformationArtifact, public workflow `ProtocolEdition` is a VersionedState. Important edge seam: catalog uses `HAS_PROTOCOL_STEP`, whereas round 0008 prose/query and the live graph use `HAS_STEP`. Freeze the canonical contract with an explicit compatibility decision; do not let independently generated fragments choose both without explaining their relationship.

Protocol steps must connect to people/roles, products or exact formulation versions where required, compounds/materials, devices/tests, activities and source/evidence assertions. The live `StepSubstance` union (`Compound | CompoundForm | Product | FoodItem | FoodProduct`) and `StepInstrument` (`Device | LabTest | Exposure`) are starting seams: ChemicalSubstance/IngredientMaterial identity reconciliation is already accepted; no worker should recreate Compound as a rival canonical substance. Public authorship or prescribed operator roles are not private execution participants. Step-level evidence is CQ-PR-06 (Research frontier): evidence for one component does not establish the effectiveness of the combined protocol.

Shared protocol templates/research descriptions and public source-attributed results remain distinct from personal adoption/execution. Round 0008 and proposal-index §3 forbid all private-personal nodes/properties/edges/private uids in the shared graph (INV-506). Public `Observation` belongs to protocols and is an InformationArtifact; private `PersonalMeasurement`, `ProtocolInUse`, `ProtocolAdoptionVersion`, deviations and decisions live only in the separate private context store. A source describing a public person's practice remains a sourced public statement; it does not license ingestion of private user health records. Adoption does not prove adherence, and public before/after reports do not prove efficacy.

## Standard output and synthesis requirements for these workers

Each worker returns a source packet with exact locator, retrieval status/completeness, authority scope and date; inventory of relevant current/catalog types; recommended nodes/properties/edges/edge properties with archetype/privacy/cardinality; CQ/query/failure map; identity/version/time rules; assessment versus source assertion distinctions; positive and negative fixtures with expected rows; unresolved ownership requests; ingestion, storage, deletion, rights and operational implications. State unsupported/unmeasured/not-reported/unknown rather than invent values. Research must decide modeling properties rather than produce a general survey.

Work waves: (1) freeze identity, assertion, temporal, provenance, quantity and privacy interfaces and register owners; (2) these four workers research/model concurrently with their substantive-domain peers; (3) joint seam review on narrative-rendition-assets, study/workflow protocol, actors/roles, quantities/versions and public/private observation; (4) adversarial fixtures and synthesis. Narrative work must not be relegated to decorative-media cleanup.

Fable 5.1 must integrate actual full type definitions into the requested complete executable schema, resolve every conflicting shared definition and edge seam, preserve forbidden implications, and report decisions still requiring user input. Pin the GraphQL library and Neo4j target edition/minor before validating directive support and physical enforcement. Verify with official documentation in the future research turn; this repo-only review does not claim current external documentation verification. Run representative fixtures in an isolated supported DB where available and report exact execution scope. Library parse success cannot prove physical cardinality, privacy, rights, bitemporal exclusivity or application rules; unsupported database constraints require explicit application validation and portability/gap documentation.

User/operator inputs can wait until implementation dependencies arise: actual availability of Fable 5.1; runtime/library/Neo4j edition/minor; supported isolated DB and retrieval tools; rights policy/use vocabulary; private-store/legal retention and aggregate release policy. They do not block domain classification. No paid service, live DB mutation or alternate synthesizer is presumed.
